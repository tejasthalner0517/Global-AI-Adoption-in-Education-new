
# Global AI Adoption in Education

## Project Overview
This capstone project explores global AI adoption in education through data visualization. It presents comparisons of school AI adoption, student and teacher usage, rural and urban AI usage, and average daily AI usage.

## Project Objectives
- Explore AI adoption patterns across countries.
- Compare student and teacher AI usage.
- Examine differences between rural and urban AI usage.
- Visualize AI adoption trends over time.
- Present the findings through an interactive Tableau dashboard and Story.

## Dataset
**File:** `Global AI in Education.csv`

The dataset contains country-level educational AI adoption and usage indicators, including year, school AI adoption, student AI usage, teacher AI usage, rural and urban AI usage, and average daily AI usage minutes.

## Visualizations
The Tableau project includes:
1. Average School AI Adoption by Country
2. AI Adoption Trends Over Time
3. Average AI Usage: Students vs Teachers
4. Rural vs Urban AI Usage
5. Average Daily AI Usage Across Countries

The dashboard includes a Year filter, and the Tableau Story organizes the visualizations into a sequence of story points.
